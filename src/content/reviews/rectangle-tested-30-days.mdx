## Install

`brew install --cask rectangle` and you are done — no account, no license nag, no
"pro" tier. First launch asks for accessibility permissions, which is exactly as
it should be for a window manager.

## First run

The defaults are sane: drag to edges to snap, or learn the shortcuts gradually.
The center snapping zones are a nice touch that paid tools charge for.

## Daily use, 30 days in

- Keyboard shortcuts (`⌃⌥ ←` for left half, etc.) became muscle memory within a week.
- It stays out of the way: roughly 40 MB of RAM and no noticeable CPU.
- Drag-to-snap feels native rather than bolted on.

## Where it falls short

No window "groups" or saved layouts, and multi-monitor edge cases (menu bar
displays, Spaces transitions) occasionally need a nudge. Power users who live in
tiling layouts may prefer AeroSpace.

## Verdict

For everyone else — and I mean everyone who has ever dragged a window to the
side of the screen — Rectangle is the answer. 8.5/10.
